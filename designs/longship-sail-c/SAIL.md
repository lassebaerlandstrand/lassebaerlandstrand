# Sail C: the world tree

A red striped sail with a cream border cord, and couched in the same cream wool a complete binary tree of depth 3: one root, two children, four grandchildren, eight leaves.

It's Yggdrasil, the Norse world tree, drawn the way computer scientists draw trees: root at the top, leaves at the bottom. Anyone who has drawn a heap, a search tree or a recursion tree on a whiteboard will recognise it. To everyone else it is a branching ornament on a Viking sail.

Only the sail differs from `designs/longship/`. Rebuild with `python3 generate.py` (`TREE_DEPTH` sets the depth; 3 is the most that still reads at 830 px).
